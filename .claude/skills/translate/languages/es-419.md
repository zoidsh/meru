# Spanish, Latin America (`es-419`)

Latin American Spanish follows Apple's conventions in macOS Mail and AppKit for `es_419` for address, label form, capitalization, punctuation and the ellipsis. Spain has its own catalog, `es`; the two never borrow from each other. Its words follow two rules:

- **An OS label takes its platform's word.** An entry whose `msgctxt` is `macOS`, `Windows` or `Linux` is a label the operating system names its own way, such as the File menu or Paste. A label only macOS shows, such as the Window menu or Hide Others, is an OS label too and carries `macOS` alone. `macOS` takes Apple's word (Apple Mail's for a mail menu item), `Windows` Microsoft's and `Linux` GNOME's, from [Platform words](#platform-words) below. Only the word changes: address, capitalization, punctuation and the ellipsis stay this sheet's on every platform.
- **Everything else takes the house word**, from this sheet and the glossary, by the glossary's [source order](../glossary.md#where-a-word-comes-from): Gmail's word for a term Gmail also shows, Google's for Google's names, and in Meru's own copy Chrome's word where Chrome has the term, else the apps' word the glossary counts and marks agent-chosen, with its tally. Apple is never the authority for a house word; its macOS apps are one vote of seven when the glossary counts the apps' word. Meru's own copy reads the same on every platform, and each rule in `es-419.json` gives its word's source.

Where this sheet and a habit from other software disagree, this sheet wins.

## Address

_tú_, with _tu_ and _te_: _¿Quieres …?_, _tu cuenta_. Never _vos_, _ustedes_ or _usted_. Chrome addresses the user the same way.

## Buttons and menu items

The infinitive, as in `es`: _Responder_, _Borrar_, _Guardar_, _Cancelar_, _Cerrar pestaña_. The confirming OK button is under [Common labels](#common-labels).

## Capitalization

Sentence style: only the first word and proper nouns take a capital. A label that opens something further ends in an ellipsis with no space before it: _Configuración…_, _Agregar cuenta…_.

## Settings

_Configuración_, as Chrome writes it too. The app-menu item with `msgctxt` `macOS` is _Configuración…_, as AppKit writes it; with `Windows` or `Linux` it takes Microsoft's or GNOME's word, without the ellipsis the English lacks.

## Access keys

An `&` in the English marks the access key on Windows and Linux. Keep one `&` inline, before a letter of the Spanish word: the English letter where the word has it, else the first free letter, one no other label in the same menu uses. macOS drops the `&`. The labels whose word has the English letter claim it first, in menu order; the rest then take their first free letter, in menu order.

The page and text-field context menu, by that policy, on Windows: `&Learn Spelling` _Aprender pa&labra_, `&Search with Google` _Bu&scar con Google_, `Cu&t` _Cor&tar_, `&Copy` _&Copiar_, `&Paste` _&Pegar_, `Paste and &Match Style` _Pegar con el &mismo formato_, `Select &All` _Seleccion&ar todo_, `Sa&ve Image As…` _&Guardar imagen como…_, `Copy Lin&k` _Cop&iar vínculo_, `Cop&y Image` _Copia&r imagen_, `C&opy Image Address` _C&opiar dirección de la imagen_. On Linux, where its words differ: `Paste and &Match Style` _Pegar con el &mismo estilo_.

## Plural categories

`one`, `many` and `other`. `many` is for large round numbers (1,000,000), so its text is usually the same as `other`'s: `{count, plural, one {# pestaña} many {# pestañas} other {# pestañas}}`. 0 takes `other`.

## Terms that stay English

Gmail, Google Workspace, Meru, Meru Pro and Pro.

## Traps

- **Latin America's vocabulary and tense.** _computadora_, _ingresar_, _Agregar_; the simple past: _No se pudo …_. Never Spain's _ordenador_, _introducir_, _Añadir_ or _No se ha podido …_. Settings is _Configuración_ in both catalogs, never Apple's _Ajustes_. Chrome's Latin American Spanish agrees on every one.
- **Punctuation.** A question or exclamation opens with ¿ or ¡. Quotation marks are “…” (U+201C, U+201D), as Apple writes them, never straight `"…"`, which Chrome mostly uses, or «…». The ellipsis is the single character …, straight after the word with no space, never three periods.
- **Numbers and units.** A plain space parts a number from its unit, as Chrome mostly writes it, never a no-break space: _5 minutos_, _# días_.
- **Placeholders and articles.** _el_, _la_ and _del_ change with the value. Put the placeholder after a noun (_la cuenta {name}_).
- **Gendered forms.** Rewrite around the person rather than use _o/a_ or _@_.
- **House words, as Chrome writes them.** A link is a _vínculo_, never an _enlace_ (_Copiar vínculo_). A bookmark is a _favorito_, never a _marcador_. An extension is an _extensión_. To pin a tab is _fijar_ and to unpin it _dejar de fijar_, never _anclar_; a pinned tab is a _pestaña fija_. To delete is _borrar_ (_Borrar búsqueda guardada_), except Gmail's own Delete action, which keeps Gmail's _Eliminar_ from the glossary. To remove something from a list is _quitar_ (_Quitar cuenta_). The download history is the _historial de descargas_, the tab strip the _barra de pestañas_, the launcher the _selector_, the default browser the _navegador predeterminado_, a password manager an _administrador de contraseñas_ and a passkey a _llave de acceso_. Do Not Disturb is Apple's _No molestar_, never Chrome's _No interrumpir_. Restarting is _reiniciar_ and an update an _actualización_. The `es-419.json` rules beside this sheet give Chrome's count for each.
- **macOS's own elements.** The menu bar, the Dock, Finder and System Settings take Apple's names in Meru's own copy too, since they name Apple's interface and show only on macOS: _barra de menús_ (ControlCenterSettings.appex `Localizable` `Show in Menu Bar`), _Dock_ (DesktopSettings.appex `Localizable` `Dock`), _Finder_ and _Configuración del Sistema_ (System Settings `InfoPlist` `CFBundleDisplayName`).

## Error messages

Say what happened, without _Lo sentimos_ or _Por favor_: _No se pudo agregar la cuenta._, as Apple writes _No se pudo renombrar el buzón “%1$@”._

## Common labels

House words for everyday actions, as Chrome writes its labels, in this sheet's label form. A string that names something Gmail also shows, such as the Undo button in the message-sent toast, takes Gmail's word from the glossary instead, and an entry with a platform `msgctxt` takes [its platform's word](#platform-words). Delete and Remove are two words, as Chrome writes them: Remove account never reads as deleting it. The labels:

- Cancel _Cancelar_, Close _Cerrar_, Delete _Borrar_, Remove _Quitar_, Add _Agregar_, Save _Guardar_, Edit _Editar_, Open _Abrir_, Show _Mostrar_, Hide _Ocultar_, Quit _Salir_, Reload _Volver a cargar_, Try Again _Volver a intentar_, Done _Listo_, Learn More… _Más información…_
- OK is two messages. A button Meru draws is Chrome's _Aceptar_ (6 of its 7 OK labels). A native dialog's OK has a platform `msgctxt` and takes each platform's word from [Platform words](#platform-words): Apple's _OK_ on macOS, _Aceptar_ on Windows and Linux.
- Close Tab _Cerrar pestaña_, Close Other Tabs _Cerrar las demás pestañas_, Pin Tab _Fijar pestaña_, Unpin _Dejar de fijar_, Show Sidebar _Mostrar barra lateral_, Copy Link _Copiar vínculo_, Mark as Read _Marcar como leído_, Add Account… _Agregar cuenta…_
- In a sentence, try again is _vuelve a intentarlo_: _Conéctate y vuelve a intentarlo._
- _Escribiste “%@”, pero parece que olvidaste agregar un documento. ¿Quieres enviar el mensaje de todos modos?_

## Platform words

The word each platform uses for every label with a platform `msgctxt`. A label keeps its English placeholders and access key as the rules above say; the table gives the words.

`es-419.json` beside this sheet holds the same words by `msgctxt` and `msgid`, with the banned words, pinned terms and access-key style that `check.ts` holds a catalog against. A test keeps it and this table in step: a cell's words are the italic spans that open each `;`-separated part.

Sources: **AppKit** and **Mail** are the `.loctable` files in `/System/Library/Frameworks/AppKit.framework/Versions/C/Resources` and `/System/Applications/Mail.app/Contents/Resources` on macOS 27, named by table and key, as are **Dictionary** and **Screen Sharing**, in `/System/Applications/Dictionary.app` and `/System/Applications/Utilities/Screen Sharing.app`; **Software Update** is `/System/Library/ExtensionKit/Extensions/SoftwareUpdateSettingsExtension.appex/Contents/Resources/Localizable.loctable`; all for Apple's `es_419`. **TBX** is the Microsoft Terminology Collection, `SPANISH.tbx`, by `termEntry` id: Microsoft ships one Spanish, whose terms here are marked for Latin American countries and Spain alike. **gtk4**, **nautilus** and **gedit** (GNOME Text Editor) are GNOME's `es.po` for each, by `msgid` (and `msgctxt` where it has one), since GNOME has no Latin American Spanish. _Inferred_ marks a word no source gives for that label, built from the source words named.
