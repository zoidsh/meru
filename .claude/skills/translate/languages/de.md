# German (`de`)

German follows Apple's conventions in macOS Mail and AppKit for address, label form, capitalization, punctuation and the ellipsis. Its words follow two rules:

- **An OS label takes its platform's word.** An entry whose `msgctxt` is `macOS`, `Windows` or `Linux` is a label the operating system names its own way, such as the File menu or Paste. A label only macOS shows, such as the Window menu or Hide Others, is an OS label too and carries `macOS` alone. `macOS` takes Apple's word (Apple Mail's for a mail menu item), `Windows` Microsoft's and `Linux` GNOME's, from [Platform words](#platform-words) below. Only the word changes: address, capitalization, punctuation and the ellipsis stay this sheet's on every platform.
- **Everything else takes the house word**, from this sheet and the glossary: Meru's own copy reads the same on every platform. A term Gmail also shows takes Gmail's word from the glossary.

Where this sheet and a habit from other software disagree, this sheet wins.

## Address

_du_, lowercase, with _dein_, _dir_ and _dich_ lowercase too: _Möchtest du … deinem Postfach_. Never _Sie_, _Ihr_ or _Ihnen_ for the user. _Sie_ was weighed against _du_ and rejected; it is not a choice a translation makes.

## Buttons and menu items

The infinitive, object first: _Antworten_, _Löschen_, _Abbrechen_, _Als gelesen markieren_, _Tab schließen_. Never an imperative (_Lösche_, _Schließe_).

## Capitalization

Sentence style, with nouns capitalized as German always does. A label that opens something further ends in an ellipsis after a no-break space (U+00A0): _Einstellungen …_, _Konto hinzufügen …_.

## Settings

_Einstellungen_. The app-menu item with `msgctxt` `macOS` is _Einstellungen …_, as AppKit writes it; with `Windows` or `Linux` it is _Einstellungen_, since the English has no ellipsis.

## Access keys

An `&` in the English marks the access key on Windows and Linux. Keep one `&` inline, before a letter of the German word, the English's letter where the German has it: `Cu&t` _&Ausschneiden_, `&Paste` _&Einfügen_. Two labels in the same menu never share a letter. macOS drops the `&`.

## Plural categories

`one` and `other`. Every plural carries exactly these two, `=0` and other exact matches aside: `{count, plural, one {# Tab} other {# Tabs}}`.

## Terms that stay English

Gmail, Google Workspace, Meru, Meru Pro and Pro, never inflected inside the word. A compound joins with a hyphen: _Gmail-Einstellungen_, _Workspace-App_, _Meru-Pro-Funktionen_.

## Traps

- **Placeholders and articles.** An article or adjective before a placeholder must agree with every value it can take, which it usually cannot. Put the placeholder after a noun that carries the case (_im Konto {name}_), or in its own phrase, never _der {name}_.
- **Gendered forms.** Address with _du_ avoids most of them; where a person is described, rewrite around the gender.
- **House words.** _Benachrichtigung_, never _Mitteilung_, for a notification. _Konto_, plural _Konten_, never _Account_, for an account. _Speichern_, never _Sichern_, for keeping something; _Sichern_ is only Apple's word in a `macOS` label such as _Sichern unter …_. _Schaltfläche_ for an on-screen button, such as the bookmarks button: _Lesezeichen-Schaltfläche_. A standalone _Taste_ reads as a keyboard key and means only that, as in the key names _Umschalttaste_ and _Control-Taste_.
- English quotation marks become „…“ (U+201E, U+201C).

## Error messages

Say what happened, without _leider_, _Entschuldigung_ or _Bitte_: _Das Konto konnte nicht hinzugefügt werden._, as Apple writes _Das Postfach „%1$@“ konnte nicht umbenannt werden._

## Common labels

House words for everyday actions, most of them Apple's from Mail's `MainMenu`, `Alerts` and `Localizable` tables and AppKit's `MenuCommands`. A string that names something Gmail also shows, such as the Undo button in the message-sent toast, takes Gmail's word from the glossary instead, and an entry with a platform `msgctxt` takes [its platform's word](#platform-words):

- Cancel _Abbrechen_, OK _OK_, Close _Schließen_, Delete _Löschen_, Remove _Entfernen_, Add _Hinzufügen_, Save _Speichern_, Edit _Bearbeiten_, Open _Öffnen_, Show _Einblenden_, Hide _Ausblenden_, Quit _Beenden_, Reload _Neu laden_, Try Again _Erneut versuchen_, Learn More… _Weitere Infos …_
- Close Tab _Tab schließen_, Close Other Tabs _Andere Tabs schließen_, Pin Tab _Tab fixieren_, Show Sidebar _Seitenleiste einblenden_, Copy Link _Link kopieren_, Mark as Read _Als gelesen markieren_
- _Möchtest du diese Nachricht wirklich löschen?_ (Are you sure you want to delete this message?)
- _Du kannst Anhänge nicht bewegen, solange sie nicht geladen wurden. Möchtest du diesen Anhang laden?_

## Platform words

The word each platform uses for every label with a platform `msgctxt`. A label keeps its English placeholders and access key as the rules above say; the table gives the words.

`de.json` beside this sheet holds the same words by `msgctxt` and `msgid`, with the banned words, pinned terms and access-key style that `check.ts` holds a catalog against. A test keeps it and this table in step: a cell's words are the italic spans that open each `;`-separated part, and the space before an ellipsis stands for the catalog's no-break space.

Sources: **AppKit** and **Mail** are the `.loctable` files in `/System/Library/Frameworks/AppKit.framework/Versions/C/Resources` and `/System/Applications/Mail.app/Contents/Resources` on macOS 27, named by table and key, as are **Dictionary** and **Screen Sharing**, in `/System/Applications/Dictionary.app` and `/System/Applications/Utilities/Screen Sharing.app`; **Software Update** is `/System/Library/ExtensionKit/Extensions/SoftwareUpdateSettingsExtension.appex/Contents/Resources/Localizable.loctable`; **TBX** is the Microsoft Terminology Collection, `GERMAN.tbx`, by `termEntry` id; **gtk4**, **nautilus** and **gedit** are GNOME's `de.po` for each, by `msgid` (and `msgctxt` where it has one). _Inferred_ marks a word no source gives for that label, built from the source words named.
